A NEW ROUTE INTO PRIVATE MARKETS

Invest together.

Raftt is building infrastructure that enables individuals to invest collectively in startups and other private assets.

Our focus goes beyond the investment itself: AI supports diligence, follows execution and helps assess evidence against agreed milestones. Capital can then be released in stages as those milestones are verified.

THE INFRASTRUCTURE UNDERNEATH

Clear rules.
Traceable movements.

Raftt plans to use stablecoins and programmable contracts on Solana to manage staged capital deployment and record transactions. Project documents, agreed terms and verification processes connect those transactions to real-world delivery.

What does the AI do?

It supports diligence and monitoring by organizing information and identifying points for review. Milestone decisions follow agreed rules and verification processes; AI does not guarantee project outcomes.

Why release capital in stages?

Each release funds a defined part of the plan. Investors can follow the evidence and progress associated with each stage.

Can I invest right now?

Raftt is in development. This page presents the planned product; live opportunities and investor access are not yet available.
